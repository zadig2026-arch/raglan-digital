## The brief

A French painters' collective based in Royan (Charente-Maritime) had outgrown their old blog. The structure was solid but the surface had aged, and any change beyond a colour swap required hands-on help. They wanted a site that put the artwork forward and let members publish without friction.

## Key decisions

**Sanity as the editing surface.** The president — and anyone on the committee — can publish a new article (a competition announcement, an outing recap, an exhibition report), add or remove photos in a member's gallery, create a new gallery when a member joins, or update the agenda. All from a clean web dashboard. What stays mine is anything structural: new top-level rubrique, charter changes, new page type.

**Two domains, French registrar.** `.fr` primary, `.tech` backup, both at OVH per client preference. DNS and email handled there too.

**Local SEO from day one.** Google Business Profile verified, structured data wired, Search Console hooked up before launch. Twenty-eight Profile views in the first month — for a hyper-local arts collective, that's signal, not noise.

## Outcome

The site went live on the 13th of April 2026. The Facebook and Instagram launch post read: *"L'art de Royan vit désormais en ligne."*

Five rubriques — Accueil · Nos photographes · Expositions · Concours · La vie du club — and a growing roster of member galleries, all editable in-house. The collective's announcement reach now lives on a domain they own outright, not on a third-party blog tool.
