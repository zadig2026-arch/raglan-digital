# A Simple Content Strategy That Actually Works

"I know I should post more but I don't have time."

I hear this from every business owner. Here's the thing: you don't need to post every day. You need to post the right things, consistently.

## The 3-2-1 Rule

Every week, aim for:
- **3 value posts** — Tips, behind-the-scenes, how-tos
- **2 social proof posts** — Customer reviews, finished work, testimonials
- **1 promotional post** — Your offer, a deal, a call to action

That's 6 posts per week. But honestly? Even 3 per week is better than nothing.

## Content Ideas That Work

### For Cafes & Restaurants
- Today's special (photo)
- Behind the scenes in the kitchen
- Customer review screenshot
- "Meet the team" post
- Seasonal menu announcement

### For Trades
- Before/after photos
- Time-lapse of a job
- Customer testimonial
- Tool or technique tip
- "What we're working on today"

### For Retail
- New arrivals
- Staff picks
- Customer wearing/using your product
- Behind the scenes of ordering/stocking
- Sale or promotion

### For Services
- FAQ answer
- Quick tip in your expertise
- Client result or testimonial
- Day in the life
- Common mistakes people make

## The Secret: Batch It

Don't create content every day. Pick one hour per week:

1. Take 5-10 photos
2. Write captions for the week
3. Schedule them using Meta Business Suite (free)

Done. One hour, whole week covered.
