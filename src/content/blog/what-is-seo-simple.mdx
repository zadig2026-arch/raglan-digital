# What Is SEO? Explained Like You're Not A Nerd

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization. But that doesn't help, does it?

## In Plain English

SEO is everything you do to make Google show your business when people search for what you offer.

That's it. That's the whole thing.

## Why It Matters

When someone in Hamilton searches "best cafe near me," Google decides which businesses to show. SEO is how you tell Google: "Hey, I'm a cafe, I'm in Hamilton, and I'm worth showing."

If you don't do any SEO, Google has to guess. And Google's guesses aren't always in your favour.

## The Three Types of SEO

### 1. On-Page SEO
What's on your website. Your page titles, descriptions, headings, and content. This is the easiest to control.

### 2. Local SEO
Your Google Business Profile, your address consistency across the web, your reviews. Critical for businesses that serve a local area.

### 3. Technical SEO
How fast your site loads, whether it works on mobile, whether Google can read it properly. You usually need someone technical for this.
