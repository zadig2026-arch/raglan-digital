# Instagram Tips That Actually Work for Raglan Businesses

Instagram can feel overwhelming when you're running a business. Everyone says you need to post Reels, use trending audio, dance on camera... but does any of that actually work for a local cafe, shop, or tradesperson?

Here's what actually moves the needle for small businesses in Raglan.

## Tip #1: Show the Real Raglan

Your biggest advantage over any big brand is your location. Raglan is beautiful, and people love seeing it. Tie your content to the place:

- Morning coffee with an ocean view
- Your workshop with the bush in the background
- Sunset from your shopfront
- Rainy day vibes at your cafe

This content performs because people have an emotional connection to Raglan.

## Tip #2: People Over Products

The most engaging posts for local businesses are almost always about people — your team, your customers (with permission), yourself.

Introduce your staff. Share why you started your business. Show the humans behind the brand. Raglan is a community, and community is built on personal connection.

## Tip #3: Stories > Feed for Daily Content

Don't stress about having a perfect feed. Use Stories for your daily, casual content:

- Behind the scenes
- Quick updates
- Polls and questions
- Sharing customer tags and mentions

Save your feed for your best content — things you'd be happy to have on your profile in 6 months.

## Tip #4: Use Location Tags Religiously

Every post, every Story — add the Raglan location tag. People browse location tags when they're visiting or thinking about visiting. Free discovery.

## Tip #5: Engage Before You Post

Spend 5 minutes before posting engaging with other Raglan accounts. Like, comment, reply to Stories. The algorithm rewards accounts that are actively social, not just broadcasting.

## Tip #6: Collaborate with Other Local Businesses

Cross-promotion is powerful in a small community. Tag other Raglan businesses, create content together, run joint promotions. Everyone's audience grows.

## Tip #7: Don't Chase Trends — Be Consistent

Consistency beats virality for local businesses. A post every two days is better than posting 10 times in one week and then going silent for a month.

Pick a schedule you can maintain and stick with it.
