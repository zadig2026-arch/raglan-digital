# You're Probably Paying Too Much For Your Website

I keep hearing the same story from NZ business owners: "I paid $6,000 for my website and I'm not even happy with it."

## Why Agencies Charge So Much

It's not because the work is worth $6,000. It's because agencies have:
- Office rent
- Multiple employees
- Project managers
- Account managers
- Sales teams

You're not paying for a better website. You're paying for their overhead.

## What A Website Actually Costs To Build

A professional one-page site for a small business takes me about 2-3 days of focused work. A 5-page site takes about a week.

The tools are free or cheap. The hosting is $5-15/month. The domain is $15/year.

What you're paying for is the skill and experience to make it look good, load fast, and rank on Google. That doesn't require an agency.

## What's A Fair Price?

For a NZ small business:
- **One-page site**: $500 - $800
- **5-page business site**: $1,000 - $1,500
- **Full site with e-commerce**: $2,000 - $3,000

If someone quotes you $8,000 for a basic site, they're charging for their conference room.
