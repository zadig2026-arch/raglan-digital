# 4 Tools To Check Your Website Health

I built a set of tools that let you check your website's health in about 5 minutes. No sign-up, no email, no catch. Here's what each one does and how to use them.

## 1. SEO Audit

**What it does:** Checks your URL for HTTPS, domain quality, and basic SEO signals.

**How to use it:** Enter your website URL → Get a score out of 100 → See what's passing and what's failing.

**[Run the SEO Audit →](/tools/seo-audit)**

## 2. Speed Test

**What it does:** Measures your site's loading speed using Core Web Vitals metrics.

**How to use it:** Enter your URL → See your performance score → Get specific recommendations for each metric.

**[Run the Speed Test →](/tools/speed-checker)**

## 3. Meta Tag Generator

**What it does:** Writes optimized title tags and meta descriptions for your Google listing.

**How to use it:** Enter your business name, type, location, and keywords → Get multiple options with character counts and Google previews.

**[Use the Meta Generator →](/tools/meta-generator)**

## 4. Digital Presence Checklist

**What it does:** Walks you through every aspect of your online presence — website, Google, social media, directories, reviews.

**How to use it:** Go through each item → Check off what you have → See your completion percentage.

**[Start the Checklist →](/tools/digital-checklist)**
