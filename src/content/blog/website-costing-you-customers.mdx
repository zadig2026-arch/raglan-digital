# Your Website Is Probably Losing You Customers

Your website is supposed to work for you 24/7. But for a lot of businesses, it's actually doing the opposite. Here's how to tell.

## 1. It Takes More Than 3 Seconds To Load

53% of visitors leave if your site is slow. That's not an opinion — it's data. [Test your speed here](/tools/speed-checker).

## 2. It Doesn't Work On Mobile

Pull out your phone right now and open your website. Is the text readable? Can you tap the buttons easily? If you're pinching to zoom, you have a problem.

## 3. There's No Clear Way To Contact You

If someone has to hunt for your phone number or email, they'll just Google someone else. Your contact info should be visible on every single page.

## 4. It Looks Like It Was Built In 2015

Design trends change. If your site has stock photos, a slider, tiny text, and a sidebar — it looks dated. People subconsciously judge your business by your website.

## 5. You Can't Find It On Google

Search your business name. If you're not on the first page, something is wrong with your SEO. Run my [SEO Audit](/tools/seo-audit) to find out what.
